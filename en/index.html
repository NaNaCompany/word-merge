    Getting started

    1. Choose at least two documents.
    2. Use the arrow buttons to arrange the files.
    3. Merge the documents, then download the new file.

    Paragraphs, tables, images, page sizes and sections are copied. Each document starts on a new page. Overall themes, default fonts and some fields depend on the first document and your editor; check the output.

    Questions & limits

    How to use

    Only standard DOCX files are supported, not .doc, HWP or PDF. Save as .docx in Word or LibreOffice first. Renaming an extension is not conversion.

    Questions & limits

    Paragraphs, tables, images, page sizes and sections are copied. Each document starts on a new page. Overall themes, default fonts and some fields depend on the first document and your editor; check the output.

    Questions & limits

    Encrypted files, macros, digital signatures, editing restrictions, tracked changes, comments and embedded objects are unsupported. Footnotes or endnotes containing images or links are also unsupported. Prepare a clean copy in Word first.

    Privacy & use

    Files and input stay in your browser. File contents, file names and QR values are not uploaded or sent as analytics events. Google Analytics, Tag Manager, AdSense and Cloudflare may receive ordinary connection data for statistics and advertising.

    Keep your originals and check the result before using important documents or printing QR codes. Contact NaNa Lab
